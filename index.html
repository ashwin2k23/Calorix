How BMI is calculated
BMI = weight(kg) ÷ height(m)²
= 78 ÷ (1.75)² = 25.5
BMI is a screening tool — not a direct fat measure. It does not account for muscle mass, bone density, or age. Athletes may show high BMI despite being lean.

Mifflin-St Jeor BMR Formula
Men: BMR = (10 × kg) + (6.25 × cm) − (5 × age) + 5
= (10×78) + (6.25×175) − (5×28) + 5 = 1,852
TDEE = BMR × Activity Factor. Subtract 500 kcal/day for ~0.5 kg/week loss. Never go below 1,200 kcal/day (1,000 for women) without medical supervision.
